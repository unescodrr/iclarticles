ICL Research Assistant: User guide

ICL Open Access Book Series “Progress in Landslide Research and Technology” · November 2026

The ICL Research Assistant is an AI-powered chatbot for the International Consortium on Landslides (ICL) Open Access Book Series on Progress in Landslide Research and Technology (PLRT). It helps you find relevant research, understand key findings and see how topics connect across the series, without searching every volume by hand. Answers are based on the articles of the series and always cite their sources, so you can check every statement in the original research. It currently covers the 202 articles of Volumes 1 to 4 (2022 to 2025); new issues are added as they are published.

Quick start

  1. Open the assistant with the round chat icon in the bottom-right corner of the series webpage.
  2. Ask a question in plain language, in any of 12 languages, or choose a starting point.
  3. Check the article behind each statement with the numbered citations and Show Sources.

What is new in November 2026

  • Questions about the series itself: its editors, how to submit an article, the upcoming Volume 5, the Kyoto Landslide Commitment 2020 and the World Landslide Forum.
  • Complete author lists; if a surname is shared, the assistant asks which author you mean.
  • Better follow-up questions, including ones that refer back with words such as “the town” or “the method”.
  • Tested before every update on a benchmark of 120 questions in 12 languages.
  • Cookie-free, anonymous usage statistics (see Privacy).

Good to know

Answers come only from the articles of the series and are AI-generated: verify key findings in the cited articles and cite the original articles, not the assistant. The assistant does not give emergency or site-specific advice.
