Privacy

This page explains what the Keyword Mapping page and the ICL Research Assistant record, and why. It was last updated on 19 November 2026.

What is recorded

  • That the page was visited, the website you came from (its name only, not the full address), any campaign tag in the link (for example from a QR code), your country, the type of device, browser and operating system, and your browser's language.
  • How the page is used: clicks on the keyword maps, the issue selected, opening the assistant, suggested questions, links followed, and the time the page was in use.
  • Questions asked to the Research Assistant, how they were answered (for example which articles were cited), and any thumbs up or down with its reason or comment.

What is not recorded

  • No cookies are used, and nothing is stored in your browser.
  • Your IP address is not stored. To count visitors, a code is made from your IP address, your browser and a random value that changes every day; the random value is deleted after the day, so the code cannot be traced back to you or linked to your visits on other days.
  • No city or precise location, no name or e-mail address, and no data is shared with advertisers or sold.
  • If your browser sends "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control", no visitor code is made at all.

Please do not type personal information into the Research Assistant.

Why

To understand how the page and the Research Assistant are used, which topics people look for, and where answers can be improved.

Where it is kept and for how long

The data is kept in a Cloudflare database used only for this service and is deleted automatically after 13 months. Only the UNESCO Disaster Risk Reduction Unit team maintaining the service can see it, and only summaries are shared with the International Consortium on Landslides (ICL).

Questions

For questions about this page, contact the UNESCO Disaster Risk Reduction Unit.
